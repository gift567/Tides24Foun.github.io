Tides 24 Foundation

Tides 24 Foundation Zambia (TFZ), registered in 2024, is a community-focused Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) dedicated to improving the health and well-being of individuals living with and those at risk of HIV in Zambia.

Organisational Objectives

  • Health and Well-being
  • Sustainable Livelihoods
  • Gender Equality and Social Inclusion
  • Climate Change Adaptation
