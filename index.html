Sis to Sis for Self-Care

Sis to Sis for Self-Care

A Peer-Led Movement Supporting Pregnant and Breastfeeding Adolescent Girls and Young Women

Tides24 Foundation Zambia (TFZ) in 2025, with support from UNAIDS, designed and launched the Sis to Sis for Self-Care model — a peer-led, girl-centred, community-based approach supporting pregnant and breastfeeding adolescent girls and young women (PBF-AGYW) living with or at risk of HIV.

Sis to Sis is more than a programme. It is a safe space, a mentorship network, and a pathway to resilience.

✔ Self-care and mental well-being
✔ ART adherence and retention in care
✔ HIV prevention literacy and PrEP uptake
✔ Social empowerment and protection linkages
✔ Livelihood resilience

At its core, Sis to Sis centres on self-awareness, dignity, and resilience — integrating self-care principles, gender-transformative programming, and community accountability into one cohesive model of support.

Rather than positioning young mothers as passive recipients of care, the model recognises them as leaders, mentors, and agents of change.

📍 National Event – Zambia 🗓 1 December 2025

Health & Well-Being

TFZ Supports the National Launch of Lenacapavir on World AIDS Day 2025

On 1st December 2025, Zambia commemorated World AIDS Day under the theme “Overcoming Disruption, Transforming the AIDS Response.” The national event marked the official launch of Lenacapavir — a long-acting HIV prevention and treatment drug expanding options for pregnant and breastfeeding women and reducing the risk of mother-to-child transmission.

As part of our Health and Well-Being focus area, TFZ strengthens access to innovative, client-centered HIV prevention and treatment services for adolescent girls, young women, and pregnant and breastfeeding mothers.

TFZ’s Contribution:
  • Development of the national implementation plan
  • Contribution to SBCC material development
  • Promotion of HIV testing, care linkage & stigma reduction
  • Showcasing the Sis to Sis Peer Mentor Model
Advancing Health & Well-Being:
  • Strengthening community engagement in HIV prevention
  • Increasing visibility of PBF-AGYW
  • Promoting stigma reduction & improved adherence
  • Advocating for expanded client-friendly services
The Way Forward:
  • Promoting male engagement in HIV testing
  • Expanding community-based HIV testing services
  • Leveraging peer mentors to scale awareness

About Us

Tides 24 Foundation Zambia (TFZ), registered in 2024, is a community-focused Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) dedicated to improving the health and well-being of individuals living with and those at risk of HIV in Zambia. With a special emphasis on women, children, and young people, TFZ works to drive sustainable community health transformation by mobilizing resources and fostering resilientt communities that respond to their own health challenges.

Economic Empowerment

In 2025, Tides24 Foundation Zambia (TFZ), in partnership with the Latu Human Rights Foundation, implemented an economic empowerment initiative to strengthen the financial resilience of adolescent girls and young women serving as peer mentors under the Sis to Sis for Self-Care model. The initiative targeted 20 peer mentors from four implementation sites and focused on equipping them with practical, income-generating skills to promote self-reliance and long-term wellbeing.
