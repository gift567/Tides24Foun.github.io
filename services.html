Economic Empowerment

In 2025, Tides24 Foundation Zambia (TFZ), in partnership with the Latu Human Rights Foundation, implemented an economic empowerment initiative to strengthen the financial resilience of adolescent girls and young women serving as peer mentors under the Sis to Sis for Self-Care model. The initiative targeted 20 peer mentors from four implementation sites and focused on equipping them with practical, income-generating skills to promote self-reliance and long-term wellbeing.
