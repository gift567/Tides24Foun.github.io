Who We Are

Tides 24 Foundation Zambia (TFZ), registered in 2024, is a community-focused Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) dedicated to improving the health and well-being of individuals living with and those at risk of HIV in Zambia. With a special emphasis on women, children, and young people, TFZ works to drive sustainable community health transformation by mobilizing resources and fostering resilientt communities that respond to their own health challenges.


Our approach centres on community-driven approaches ensuring that children, adolescents, and women receive comprehensive health services, social protection, and economic support. A key strategy is the integration of self-care approaches, empowering individuals with knowledge and tools to take charge of their health, reducing dependence on overburdened healthcare facilities.


We integrate gender transformative programming in our work which ensures that women and girls, who are oftentimes the most vulnerable, receive the necessary support in areas such as education, economic opportunities, and sexual and reproductive health services. We promote development anchored and livelihood- based health innovations aimed at reducing inequalities and empowering these groups to actively participate in the development of their communities. Our advocacy efforts for improved community health outcomes envisions improved leadership, policy environment and accountability for better health, connects communities with relevant platforms, duty bearers and Policy makers at sub-national and national levels.

Organisational Objectives

Our organization is committed to enhancing comprehensive healthcare access with a focus on HIV prevention and treatment for children, women, and young people; fostering economic empowerment and resilience among adolescents, young women, and individuals living with or at risk of HIV; promoting gender-transformative and inclusive practices that empower women, youth, and marginalized groups; implementing climate-resilient strategies for adolescents, young women, and those affected by HIV; and strengthening community engagement and governance to ensure active participation of individuals living with or at risk of HIV in decision-making processes.

Our Vision

We envision a Zambia where individuals, particularly women, youth, and children living with or at risk of HIV, have access to comprehensive support systems that empower them to lead healthy and fulfilling lives. A Zambia where every individual can achieve their full potential.

Our Mission

To transform communities by empowering individuals living with or at risk of HIV through innovative, community-driven initiatives that promote health, gender equity, sustainable livelihoods, and social inclusion, fostering resilience and creating opportunities for everyone to thrive.
